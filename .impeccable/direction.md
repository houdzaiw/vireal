# Vireal v1.2 direction contract

## THESIS

Turn a backend-driven AI effect catalogue into a compact mobile cinema: inspiration is visual, while every generation decision remains explicit and truthful.

## OWN-WORLD

The Midnight Motion Catalogue. A near-black phone canvas, cool mist-cyan state signals, cinematic media, and numbered tonal fallback covers. The immutable authority is `h5/outputs/vireal-wan-video-h5/prototype/prototype_v1.2.html`.

## STORY

Browse a live category, choose an effect, upload the exact required number of images, select a server-supported duration, confirm the real coin cost, and follow the asynchronous task into Works.

## FIRST VIEWPORT

At 390px, the first viewport shows the Vireal wordmark, live balance, account entry, horizontal category rail, active category heading, and the leading cinematic effect card. Discover, Works, and Account form the only bottom destinations.
