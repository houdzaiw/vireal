# Quality bar: The Midnight Motion Catalogue

- Native device: a 390–430px low-light phone canvas, not a stretched desktop dashboard.
- Frame: one leading effect spans both columns; subsequent effects create a dense two-column stream whose length is data-driven.
- Depth: tonal panel steps, quiet translucent lines, and soft ambient shell/overlay shadows only.
- Lettering: compact bilingual sans hierarchy with tightly controlled metadata tracking and no decorative display type.
- Ornament: mist-cyan appears only for identity, selected state, truthful live values, focus, and the primary action.
- Motion: one short material ease on route entry plus restrained fallback-surface drift; reduced-motion removes both.
- Completion floor: real loading, empty, error, disabled, insufficient-balance, task, and missing-media states; no horizontal overflow at 390px.
